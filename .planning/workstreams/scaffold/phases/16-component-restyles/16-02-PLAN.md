  surfaceSheen unset so dark cards keep the exact flat color (PD-05).
- ScaffoldCard's elevated variant passes `color: palette.deepBlueCardColor` explicitly, which is also
  ScaffoldSurface's default. The sheen applies only to a surface with no caller color, the source's
  "default surface" rule.
