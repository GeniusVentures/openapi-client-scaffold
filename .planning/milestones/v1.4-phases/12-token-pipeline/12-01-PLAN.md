`ScaffoldDimens` values. A new driver, `scaffold_codegen.generators.theme_defaults`, renders the

the committed Dart fails a check (TOK-03, Dart side). This is a refactor. Behavior does not change.
Later phases (13 theme fields, 14 AA fixes, 15 midnight preset) build on this schema and driver.

Output: the driver, one template, the token namespace, six marker lines in lib/theme, a pytest
suite, and a Dart parity test.
