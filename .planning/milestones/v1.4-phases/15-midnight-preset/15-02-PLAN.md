- surface (backgrounds checked against): surfaceBase, surfaceSheet
- text, 4.5:1: accentBadgeText
- excluded, with these reasons:
  - surfaceSunken: recessed track or well that holds no text in the preset
  - scrim: modal barrier
  - accentPrimary, accentPrimaryStrong, accentSecondary, accentSecondaryStrong, accentSecondaryBright,
    accentTertiary: fill or gradient stop; its foreground pair is checked in 15-04
  - textOnAccent, textOnError: foreground on a fill, checked as a pair in 15-04
  - textTertiary: disabled label, exempt under WCAG 1.4.3
  - borderStrong: decorative hairline
  - borderControlOnAccent: edge on a translucent accent track, no consumer yet
  - statusWarning: warning fill
  - selection: text selection highlight
